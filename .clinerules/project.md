# StudyBuddy — Brand Brief

## What it is

StudyBuddy is a platform that helps university students find study partners and compatible study groups based on their university, degree, courses, schedules, and study preferences. Students use the platform to create a profile, find groups, and connect with other students.

## Palette

- Primary: #0B3954
- Accent: #087E8B — use for main buttons, important actions, and highlighted information
- Background: #F7F7F2

## Fonts

- Headings: Poppins
- Body: Open Sans

## Tone

Friendly, simple, trustworthy. Not this: it must not feel like a complicated or overly corporate university platform.

## Screens

- StudyBuddy Home (home)

## Stack, pinned

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript reading a local JSON file, styled with Bootstrap 5 loaded from a CDN. No framework, no npm, no build step.

Bootstrap 5 — two lines, both required:

```html
<!-- in <head> -->
<link href="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap@5.3.8/dist/css/bootstrap.min.css" rel="stylesheet">

<!-- just before </body> -->
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap@5.3.8/dist/js/bootstrap.bundle.min.js"></script>
